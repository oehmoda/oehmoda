## Article II — Capital Structure

2.1 **Units.** The Company's equity is denominated in **10,000,000 Units**, divided at adoption into:

| Block | Units | % | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Founder Reserve Units (Class F) | 2,500,000 | 25.0% | Five per-founder set-asides of 500,000 (5.0%) each, earned under Article V |
| Contribution Pool Units (Class C) | 6,500,000 | 65.0% | Issued solely through the Contribution Ledger System (Article VI) |
| Advisor Pool Units (Class C-A) | 200,000 | 2.0% | Advisor grants under Article VII (includes the Advisor Grant to [Professor]) |
| Unissued Reserve | 800,000 | 8.0% | Board-controlled buffer for future hires/advisors; issuable **only** through Article VI or VII mechanics |

All classes are economically identical (same per-Unit distribution and liquidation rights); they differ only in *how they are earned* and in the vesting/forfeiture overlays below. `[⚖ counsel: confirm single economic class is preserved; investors dislike bespoke economic tiers pre-financing]`

2.2 **No additional issuance outside the system.** No Units may be issued except (a) Class F Units earned under Article V, (b) Class C Units issued under the Contribution Ledger System, (c) Class C-A Units under approved Advisor Agreements, or (d) with a Supermajority Vote (Article IX).

2.3 **Capital accounts; no capital calls.** Standard capital-account maintenance; no Member is obligated to contribute capital. Voluntary cash contributions are credited through the Ledger (Article VI) — they do **not** directly purchase Units.

2.4 **Certificates.** Uncertificated; the Ledger and the Unit register are the record.

## Article IV — Governance: Board of Managers

4.1 **Manager-managed.** The Company is manager-managed by a **Board of Managers** of **three (3)** seats:

- **Seat 1 — CEO seat:** held ex officio by the CEO (Article VIII).
- **Seat 2 — Active Founder seat:** elected annually by majority vote of **Active Founders** (one vote per Active Founder, not per Unit).
- **Seat 3 — Independent seat:** an individual who is not a Member, employee, or family member of either, elected annually by a majority-in-interest of all Voting Units. Until filled, Seats 1–2 act by unanimity and must fill Seat 3 within 90 days. The Independent Manager chairs the Board.

`[⚖ Open decision: founders may prefer a 5-seat board (CEO + 2 Active-Founder seats + 2 independents) at Series A; the NVCA-style expansion mechanics should be pre-drafted]`

4.2 **Terms; removal; vacancies.** One-year terms, no term limits. Any Manager may be removed, with or without cause, by the constituency that elected that seat. Vacancies are filled for the unexpired term by the same constituency within 30 days.

4.3 **Action.** Majority of the full Board (2 of 3). Quorum is 2, which must include the Independent Manager for any Reserved Board Matter in §4.4. Regular meetings at least monthly; notice 5 business days (waivable); action by unanimous written consent permitted.

4.4 **Reserved Board Matters** (Board majority *including* the Independent Manager):
(a) adopting/changing the annual budget and any expenditure >$[25,000] outside it;
(b) setting the Rate Card and Role Assignments (Article VI);
(c) hiring/termination/compensation of anyone earning >$[100k]/yr equivalent;
(d) appointing or removing the CEO;
(e) approving Advisor Agreements;
(f) initiating or settling litigation >$[25,000];
(g) related-party transactions (interested Manager abstains).

4.5 **Member Reserved Matters** — require a **Supermajority Vote** (66⅔% of Voting Units), in addition to Board approval:
(a) amendment of this Agreement (except ministerial Ledger updates under Article VI, which require Board approval only);
(b) issuance of Units other than under Article II.2;
(c) merger, sale of all/substantially all assets, or dissolution;
(d) incurrence of indebtedness >$[100,000] aggregate;
(e) redemption of any Member's Units except as this Agreement already provides;
(f) any change to the Contribution Ledger System's formulas, rates-methodology, or multipliers (Article VI.9);
(g) conversion or reorganization of the Company **except** a Qualified Financing Conversion, which is governed by §14.2 and requires only Board approval plus a simple majority of Voting Units.

4.6 **Voting Units.** Each *issued and earned* Unit carries one vote. Unearned Class F set-asides and unallocated pools carry no votes. There are no per-capita member votes except the Active-Founder election in §4.1 and the Active Founder determinations in §5.2.

4.7 **Fiduciary duties.** Managers and officers owe the duties of loyalty and care; the Members acknowledge Massachusetts law may impose heightened good-faith duties among members of a closely held company, and this Agreement's remedies procedures (Article X) are intended to satisfy, not eliminate, those duties. `[⚖ counsel: decide how far 156C permits duty tailoring; do NOT attempt full waiver — see design doc §10]`

## Article V — Founder Reserve Units (the five 5% set-asides)

5.1 **Grant.** Each founder is allocated a **Founder Reserve of 500,000 Class F Units (5.0%)**. The Reserve is a *conditional set-aside, not a grant*: Units are earned tranche-by-tranche and any unearned tranche is **permanently forfeited** — there is no catch-up, deferral, or acceleration except §5.5.

5.2 **Earning schedule.** The Reserve is divided into **12 equal quarterly tranches of 41,666 Units** (final tranche 41,674) over the **12 calendar quarters** beginning [first day of the first full quarter after adoption]. A founder earns a tranche if and only if, for that quarter, the founder satisfied the **Active Service Standard**:
(i) at least **400 Ledger-verified hours** of Qualified Contributions (≈31 hrs/week) **or** such lower quarterly threshold (not below 300 hours) as the Board sets in advance for a Board-approved reduced-load role; **and**
(ii) achievement, certified by the Board, of at least [70]% of the founder's written quarterly objectives set under §8.4 (the Independent Manager certifies for Managers; an interested founder abstains from their own certification).

5.3 **Forfeiture.** An unearned tranche is cancelled as of quarter-end and its Units are automatically re-designated as Contribution Pool Units. A founder who fails the Active Service Standard for **three consecutive quarters** forfeits the entire remaining Reserve and is thereafter re-designated an **Inactive Founder** (§5.6).

5.4 **No retroactive Reserve.** The Reserve earns **prospectively only**. All past (Year-1) work is compensated exclusively through the Retrospective Contribution Audit (Article VI.7) on the same terms as everyone else.

5.5 **Acceleration.** Earned-to-date only, plus: on a Sale of the Company, each founder then meeting the Active Service Standard earns the next **4** tranches (single-trigger cap of 12 months); on death or Disability, the next **2** tranches. No other acceleration. `[⚖ Open decision: founders may prefer double-trigger-only; flag for negotiation]`

5.6 **Inactive Founders.** An Inactive Founder (a) keeps all *earned* Class F and Class C Units, with full economic and voting rights attached to those Units; (b) holds no reserved role, title, or Board seat by virtue of founder status; (c) does not vote in Active-Founder elections; (d) remains bound by Article XI transfer restrictions and Article XII covenants; and (e) may re-attain Active Founder status only by Board approval of a defined role with the Active Service Standard applying prospectively. **No Units are confiscated for inactivity** — inactivity only stops *future* earning. `[⚖ counsel: this design deliberately avoids freeze-out exposure under Donahue/Wilkes-line duties; see design doc §10]`

## Article VI — The Contribution Ledger System (Class C Units)

> The complete formulas, worked example, and rubric are in `plan-1-design.md` §5; this Article encodes the operative terms.

6.1 **Single system, identical terms.** All Class C Units — 65% of the Company, plus any Units forfeited into the pool — are allocated **exclusively** through this Article. Founders and employees participate on **identical terms**: same Rate Card, same multipliers, same verification, same caps. No person receives a premium rate by virtue of founder status, title, or seniority of tenure.

6.2 **Contribution Points.** Each Participant accrues Contribution Points ("Points," denominated in dollars) monthly:

- **Time:** `Points = Verified Hours × Applicable Hourly Rate × Compensation Multiplier × Quality Factor`
  - **Applicable Hourly Rate** = (annual market base salary for the *function actually performed that month*, per the Rate Card) ÷ 2,080.
  - **Compensation Multiplier** = `1 + (1 − f)`, where **f** = fraction of the Rate-Card cash rate actually paid in cash for those hours. Fully unpaid work ⇒ **2.0×**; fully market-paid work ⇒ **1.0×**.
  - **Quality Factor** = 0.8–1.2 per the rubric in §6.5.
- **Cash:** contributed cash and Board-pre-approved unreimbursed expenses ⇒ `Points = $ amount × 4.0`.
- **Property/IP:** contributed at Board-approved appraised value × 2.0. `[⚖ counsel: tax treatment of property contributions]`

6.3 **Rate Card.** A schedule of annual market base salaries by function and level — at minimum: Software Engineering (L1–L5), Engineering Management (M1–M2), Product/Business Strategy (P1–P2), Design, Operations — set annually by the Board using the median for the Boston metro from at least two independent published compensation datasets, and applied uniformly. The Rate Card and each Participant's Role Assignments are Ledger records visible to all Participants.

6.4 **Function-based valuation; the vision cap.** Hours are valued at the rate of the *function performed*, not the person's title. **Strategy/vision hours** (function P) are creditable only when tied to a written, dated artifact (strategy memo, fundraising material, signed partnership, hiring plan actually executed) and are capped at **15% of any Participant's credited hours per quarter** unless the Board pre-approves a higher cap for a designated role (e.g., CEO up to 50%). Engineering management hours require an identified managee count ≥ [3] or Board-designated management responsibility.

6.5 **Verification and Quality Factor.** Hours are logged at least monthly with references to verifiable artifacts (commits/PRs, tickets, docs, meetings with attendees). The **Compensation Committee** (the Independent Manager plus two Participants elected annually by all Participants, no two of whom may be founders) certifies each Participant's monthly log within 30 days, applying the published rubric: default Quality Factor **1.0**; 0.8 for work returned/unusable; up to 1.2 for work certified as materially exceeding the bar, with written justification required for any factor ≠ 1.0. A Participant's uncertified self-log older than 60 days lapses.

6.6 **Anti-gaming caps.** Creditable hours are capped at **60/week and 260/month** per Participant. Time on Ledger administration itself is capped at 4 hours/month. No Points for fundraising *hours* beyond the P-cap in §6.4 — fundraising success is reflected through the Company's value, not extra Points.

6.7 **Retrospective Contribution Audit (the past year).** Within 90 days of adoption, all Points for the period from inception to adoption are established one time, on the same formulas, by the following process:
 (a) each Participant (founders, employees, and any former service provider the Board identifies) submits a claimed monthly log with evidence (git history, PR reviews, design docs, calendars, correspondence);
 (b) claimed hours are capped at 60/week and must be corroborated by artifacts for at least [60]% of claimed totals; uncorroborated hours are credited at 50%;
 (c) the Compensation Committee scores all submissions in one calibrated session, founders recused from their own scoring;
 (d) the resulting Year-1 Point balances are published to all Participants, with a 15-day objection window and dispute resolution per Article X;
 (e) the audited balances are final and are the *entire* compensation for pre-adoption service — superseding the "equal partners" and any other informal understanding, per §1.1.
 Because pre-adoption work was uncompensated, the 2.0× Compensation Multiplier applies to substantially all Year-1 time. `[⚖ counsel: retroactive Units for past services are taxable compensation at current FMV — obtain a contemporaneous valuation and coordinate the issuance/83(b)-protective mechanics; see design doc §7]`

6.8 **Conversion of Points to Units.** At each quarter-end ("Allocation Date"), each Participant's **entitlement share** of the Contribution Pool = (Participant's cumulative certified Points) ÷ (all Participants' cumulative certified Points). Units are issued (or trued up) so that issued Class C Units match entitlement shares, rounded to whole Units. Units so issued compensate *work already performed* and are therefore **fully earned on issuance** (no service-based vesting), but remain subject to Articles XI–XII. The denominator is dynamic: relative shares of the pool shift as Points accrue, but only *within* the 65% pool — Class F and Class C-A holders are not diluted by Ledger activity, and vice versa. `[⚖ counsel: choose the tax-efficient issuance form — capital vs. profits interests per quarter — and the timing of formal issuance vs. book entitlement; see design doc §7]`

6.9 **Freeze.** The Ledger freezes permanently upon the earlier of (a) the initial closing of a Qualified Financing, (b) a Sale of the Company, or (c) a Supermajority Vote to freeze. On freeze, final entitlements are computed and issued; any unissued remainder of the pool and the Unissued Reserve become the successor entity's employee option pool (§14.2). Post-freeze compensation moves to cash + conventional forward-vesting grants.

6.10 **Amendments.** The Board may correct ministerial errors and publish the annual Rate Card; any change to formulas, multipliers, caps, committee composition rules, or this Article requires a Supermajority Vote (§4.5(f)).

7.2 **The [Professor] settlement.** In full satisfaction of any prior informal promise of equity, the Company will offer [Professor Name] a written Advisor Agreement providing:

## Article VIII — Officers; the CEO

8.1 **Single CEO.** The Company has one Chief Executive Officer — no co-CEOs. The CEO serves at the pleasure of the Board.

8.2 **Initial selection.** Within 45 days of adoption the Board (with the Independent Manager seated) selects the CEO by the following process: any Active Founder or senior Participant may stand; each candidate submits a written 12-month operating plan; the Board interviews all candidates and selects by majority vote, the candidate-Manager abstaining on their own candidacy.

8.3 **Removal and succession.** The Board may remove the CEO at any time by majority vote of the non-CEO Managers (both of the other two Managers, in a 3-seat Board). Removal as CEO does not, by itself, affect Units, Ledger participation, or Member status. The Board appoints an interim CEO within 15 days and re-runs §8.2 within 90 days.

8.4 **Founder roles and objectives.** Each Active Founder holds a written role description approved by the Board, with quarterly written objectives agreed in advance with the CEO (for the CEO: with the other Managers). These objectives feed §5.2(ii) and the annual review in §8.5.

8.5 **Annual review.** The Board conducts written annual reviews of the CEO and each Active Founder, delivered and archived with the Ledger.

## Article X — Dispute Resolution; Deadlock

10.1 **Ledger disputes** (scoring, certification, Rate Card application, Audit results): (a) written objection within 15 days; (b) Compensation Committee reconsideration within 15 days; (c) final, binding **expert determination** by a neutral compensation professional jointly selected (or, failing agreement in 10 days, appointed by the AAA), deciding **only** by choosing between the Committee's figure and the objector's figure ("baseball" format), costs to the non-prevailing party. No Ledger dispute may be litigated.

10.2 **Board deadlock** cannot arise on ordinary matters (3 seats, majority rule); if a seat is vacant and the remaining two Managers split on a decision that cannot await the 30-day vacancy fill, the matter is decided by majority-in-interest of Voting Units.

10.3 **Member-level deadlock** (a Reserved Matter failing to achieve the required vote twice over ≥90 days, where the Board certifies the impasse materially harms the Company): (a) mandatory executive negotiation (10 days); (b) mediation under AAA commercial rules (30 days); (c) if unresolved, any Member group holding ≥25% of Voting Units may trigger **appraised-value resolution**: an independent appraiser sets fair value; the Company (first) or the non-triggering majority (second) may elect to purchase the triggering group's Units at that value, payable up to [3] years with interest at [AFR+2%]; if neither elects, the specific proposal is deemed rejected and may not be re-proposed for 12 months. **No shotgun/Texas-shootout clause** — see design doc §4.6 for why. `[⚖ counsel: confirm enforceability and funding mechanics]`

10.4 **Everything else:** mandatory mediation, then binding arbitration (single arbitrator, AAA, seat Boston, MA law), carve-outs for injunctive relief on Articles XI–XII.

## Article XI — Transfer Restrictions

11.1 No transfer of Units except: (a) Permitted Transfers (estate-planning trusts, death), transferee bound by joinder; (b) with Board consent, subject to a **30-day Company right of first refusal**, then a 15-day pro-rata Member ROFR. Non-complying transfers are void; a permitted transferee of a non-Member receives economic rights only unless admitted under §3.2.

11.2 **Drag-along.** On a Sale approved by the Board and a Supermajority Vote, all Members must sell/vote/waive accordingly (customary limits: several liability, no restrictive covenants beyond those already agreed, pro-rata consideration).

11.3 **Company call on covenant breach.** If a Member materially breaches Article XII (finally determined under §10.4), the Company may repurchase up to [50]% of that Member's Units at the lower of cost or appraised fair value less [25]%. `[⚖ counsel: enforceability of forfeiture-for-breach in MA; calibrate to avoid penalty-clause risk]` This call does **not** apply to mere inactivity.

11.4 **No involuntary redemption of earned Units otherwise.** Earned Units may not be redeemed or cancelled without the holder's consent, except §§10.3, 11.3, and 14.2 (conversion mechanics).
