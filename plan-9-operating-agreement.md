## Article II — Definitions (principal defined terms)

- **Participant:** any Founder, Employee-Participant, or Enrolled Advisor admitted to the Contribution Ledger. All Participants accrue Contribution Points on identical terms (§5.1).
- **Founder:** the five individuals listed on Schedule F. "Founder" is a historical designation; except for the Founder Commitment Reserve (Article VII), it confers no rights, preferences, or duties.
- **Contribution Points (CP):** units of credited contribution computed under Article V.
- **Ledger Share:** a Participant's CP divided by all Participants' CP, as of the most recent Quarterly Statement.
- **Contribution Pool:** 100% of Crystallization fully-diluted equity minus (a) earned Founder Commitment Units and (b) the vested Advisor Grant.
- **Active Founder Status:** for any fiscal quarter, ≥ **[260]** Ledger-verified hours in that quarter, or service as an officer or Manager in good standing with ≥ **[195]** Ledger-verified hours.
- **Crystallization:** the event defined in §14.1.
- **Cause / Good Reason:** [counsel to draft tight conventional definitions; the Ledger's verified records (adjudicated falsification under §6.5; sustained non-acceptance of work) are designated inputs to a Cause determination].
- **Qualified Financing:** a priced equity financing of at least $**[1,000,000]**.
- **Rate Table, Evidence Standards, Decision Matrix, Leaver Matrix:** Schedules A–D.

## Article III — Members; Units; No Wage Substitution

3.4 **No new members outside the system.** Admission of any person other than through the Contribution Ledger, the Advisor Grant, or a financing approved under Article IX is a Tier-3 Reserved Matter.

## Article IV — Retroactive Settlement (the unpaid first year)

4.1 **Window.** Within **[60]** days of the Effective Date, every Participant (including each Founder, each current or former unpaid worker, and the Advisor if enrolled) may file a Retroactive Claim reconstructing, month by month, hours worked (by role), cash contributed, and unreimbursed expenses, from Company inception to the Effective Date.

4.2 **Scoring.** Claims are scored under the identical formula of Article V, subject to the Evidence Standards (Schedule B): Tier A (directly documented — repository history, authored documents, calendared meetings, bank records) credited 100%; Tier B (estimates corroborated in writing by two other Participants) credited 85%; Tier C (uncorroborated self-report) credited 50% and capped at 15 hours/week. Cash and expense claims require bank or receipt records; otherwise they are denied.

4.3 **Process.** The Contribution Committee, assisted by a neutral facilitator **[name/firm]**, scores all claims in a single pass and publishes the complete proposed retroactive ledger to all Participants simultaneously. Objections: 15 days to the facilitator; then Article XV ladder. Absent timely objection the retroactive ledger becomes final and non-reopenable.

4.4 **Character; tax.** Retroactive CP (and any units issued in respect of it) is a compensatory grant for past services, granted at current fair market value. [Counsel: contemporaneous valuation; §83 treatment; 83(b) elections within 30 days of any property transfer; withholding/employment-tax mechanics; explicit non-waiver language re wage claims.]

## Article V — The Contribution Ledger

5.1 **Identical terms.** Every Participant accrues CP under this Article on identical terms. No provision of this Agreement may create a contribution-crediting rule that distinguishes Founders from Employee-Participants. (The sole founder-specific economic provision in this Agreement is Article VII, as fixed by the Members' foundational requirements.)

5.2 **Accrual.** For each calendar month, each Participant accrues:

> CP = (Verified Hours × Applicable Rate × **2.0**)
> — where the Participant receives partial cash compensation, Verified Hours × (Applicable Rate − hourly cash rate actually paid) × 2.0, floored at zero —
> + (cash contributed × **4.0**)
> + (approved unreimbursed expenses × **4.0**)
> + (Board-pre-approved equipment or IP at appraised fair market value × **2.0**).

5.3 **Rates.** The Applicable Rate for each role/level is the 50th-percentile Boston-market total cash compensation per the Benchmark Source **[choose: Carta Total Comp | Pave | Radford; public fallback: BLS OEWS, Boston–Cambridge–Nashua metro]**, divided by 2,080. The Rate Table (Schedule A) is public to all Participants, is reset each January 1 (a Tier-2 matter), and admits no individually negotiated rates. Hours are logged against the role actually performed (engineering, management, strategy/vision, sales, operations) at that role's rate; strategy/vision hours require artifacts per Schedule B.

5.4 **Caps.** Creditable hours: ≤ **[60]**/week and ≤ **[12]**/day. Ideas without executed artifacts: 0×. Sales commissions, if adopted: [Schedule A rate schedule].

5.5 **Logging and attestation.** Weekly logging in the Company's designated system; every entry references ≥1 verifiable work item. Monthly: Participant attestation + counter-signature by the responsible functional lead.

5.6 **Statements.** Monthly individual statements (own CP, total CP, resulting Ledger Share); quarterly Company-wide statements to all Participants.

## Article VI — Contribution Committee

6.1 **Composition.** Three Participants, elected annually by all Participants voting Ledger Shares; **at least one member must be a non-Founder**; no person reviews their own entries (recusal + alternate).

6.2 **Powers (verification only).** The Committee verifies or disallows entries strictly against Schedule B. It has **no** power to scale, discount, bonus, or otherwise adjust credited amounts.

6.3 **Audit.** Monthly random audit of ≥ **[20]**% of entries plus all challenged entries.

6.4 **Appeals.** Committee determinations are appealable through Article XV; the arbitrator's review is confined to correct application of Schedule B.

6.5 **Falsification.** A knowingly false entry voids the entry and forfeits the filer's CP for that month (first offense); a second adjudicated offense constitutes Cause.

## Article VII — Founder Commitment Reserve

7.1 **Grant.** Each Founder may earn up to **5.0 percentage points** of Crystallization fully-diluted equity ("Founder Commitment Units"), in 16 equal quarterly tranches (0.3125 pp) over the 16 fiscal quarters following the Effective Date.

7.2 **Cliff.** Tranches for quarters 1–4 credit only if the Founder holds Active Founder Status at the end of quarter 4.

7.3 **Earning test.** A tranche credits if and only if the Founder maintained Active Founder Status for that quarter, determined mechanically from the verified Ledger. A missed quarter's tranche is **permanently cancelled**; cancelled percentage points augment the Contribution Pool.

7.4 **Leave.** One quarter per 16 [bracket] of documented medical/family/statutory leave pauses (does not forfeit) the schedule. [Counsel: conform to PFML and related leave-law obligations.]

7.5 **No retroactivity; no transfer.** No tranche accrues for pre-Effective-Date periods. Founder Commitment Units are personal and nontransferable; unvested tranches cancel at any termination of service per Schedule D.

## Article VIII — Advisor Grant (the Professor)

8.1 **Fixed grant.** Subject to §8.3, the Advisor receives **1.0 percentage point** of Crystallization fully-diluted equity, vesting monthly over **24** months with a **[3]**-month cliff, under a written advisor agreement terminable by either party on 10 days' notice and auto-terminating after 6 months without requested services; unvested amounts lapse to the Contribution Pool.

8.2 **Ledger enrollment.** The Advisor may additionally enroll in the Contribution Ledger for Board-pre-approved engagements (≤ **[300]** hours/year) at the Benchmark Source rate for senior ML advisory services, on terms identical to all Participants.

8.3 **Conditions precedent.** (a) Written evidence of Yale conflict-of-interest disclosure and any required approval, and conformity of the role with Yale's external-activity limits; (b) IP-provenance representation; where any contribution implicates Yale's Patent Policy or university resources, the Company's rights derive from a **Yale license** [counsel to negotiate], not personal assignment; (c) execution of the Company's confidentiality and (non-Yale) IP assignment agreement. **The grant is void ab initio if (a)–(c) are not satisfied within [90] days.**

8.4 **Governance.** The Advisor holds no Manager seat by virtue of the grant; he may chair a non-governing Scientific Advisory Board.

## Article IX — Management

9.1 **Voting.** Members/Participants vote their Ledger Shares as of the most recent Quarterly Statement [Open Decision Point 2 — counsel: mechanics of CP-weighted voting in an LLC; if Employee-Participants hold phantom (non-member) interests pre-Crystallization, specify contractual voting/consent rights or a proxy/nominee mechanism so that identical terms extend to governance].

9.2 **Manager-managed; Board of Managers (3 seats).** Seat 1: the CEO, ex officio. Seats 2–3: elected annually by Participants voting Ledger Shares; **Seat 3 must be held by a non-Founder Participant [bracket: or second at-large seat]**. Any elected Manager may be removed, with or without cause, by majority Ledger-Share vote. Quorum 2; act of the Board = majority of seats.

9.3 **CEO.** Selected and removable by Board majority (the two elected Managers acting together suffice to remove); annual confirmation vote of the Board; full-time; no supervoting or entrenchment rights.

9.4 **Officers/DRIs.** The Board appoints officers; the CEO designates functional DRIs. Significant decisions are recorded in decision memos per Schedule C.

9.5 **Decision tiers (Schedule C).**
- **Tier 1 (ordinary course):** CEO/DRIs — including spend below $**[25,000]** per item within budget.
- **Tier 2 (Board majority):** annual budget; officer hires/terminations; debt > $**[50,000]**; IP licenses out of the ordinary course; initiating litigation; annual Rate Table reset; advisor engagements over the §8.2 cap.
- **Tier 3 (Reserved Matters — ≥ 66⅔% of Ledger Shares):** amendment of this Agreement; admission of Members outside §3.4; merger, sale, dissolution; the conversion terms under Article XIV; related-party transactions; **and any amendment to Article V, Article VI, Schedule A methodology, or Schedule B, which additionally requires majority approval of Ledger Shares held by non-Founder Participants [bracket]**.

9.6 **Board sunset at financing.** At the first Qualified Financing, the Board reconstitutes per the financing documents (anticipated NVCA pattern), and §9.2's composition rules sunset.

## Article X — Departures; Schedule D (Leaver Matrix)

10.1 **Good Leaver** (termination without Cause; resignation for Good Reason; death; Disability): retains all accrued CP (converts at Crystallization); cash-multiplied CP retained in full; vested Founder Commitment Units retained; unvested cancel.

10.2 **Bad Leaver** (termination for Cause; resignation without Good Reason): (a) the Company holds an option, exercisable within **[90]** days, to repurchase the leaver's time-based CP at **1× unmultiplied imputed value** (verified hours × Applicable Rate, no multiplier), payable over **[36]** months [counsel: promissory-note terms, subordination to any financing]; (b) CP from cash contributions converts to an interest-free obligation of the Company repaid [when cash permits / at Crystallization]; (c) all unvested Founder Commitment Units and unvested Advisor Grant amounts cancel.

10.3 **Inactive without departing.** A Participant who simply ceases contributing accrues no further CP; no action is required. This Article's repurchase applies only on actual termination/resignation events.

## Article XIV — Crystallization; Conversion

14.1 **Crystallization** occurs on the earliest of: (a) immediately prior to closing of a Qualified Financing; (b) certification of two consecutive cash-flow-positive fiscal quarters; (c) the date **[30]** months after the Effective Date; (d) approval by 75% of Ledger Shares.

14.2 **Mechanics.** The Ledger closes with the then-current month; final percentages are fixed as: (Contribution Pool × each Participant's final Ledger Share) + earned Founder Commitment Units + vested Advisor Grant. The Company then converts to a Delaware corporation; fixed percentages are issued as a single class of common stock; a new option pool of **[12–15]**% is created for post-Crystallization service; the Board adopts standard 4-year/1-year-cliff vesting for all forward grants.

14.3 **Instrument events.** [Counsel: §351 analysis; §357(c)/negative-capital-account traps; 83(b) filings within 30 days for any restricted issuances; QSBS eligibility and timing memo (post-OBBBA tiered holding rules; FMV-basis step-up trade-off); no SAFEs or convertible instruments to be issued by the LLC.]

14.4 **Post-conversion covenant.** Members agree to customary post-conversion documents (voting/ROFR/co-sale as applicable) consistent with then-current NVCA norms.

## Article XV — Dispute Resolution

15.1 **Ladder.** (a) Good-faith negotiation, 15 days; (b) non-binding mediation, [JAMS/AAA], Boston, 30 days; (c) final and binding arbitration — AAA Commercial Rules, one arbitrator, seat Boston, **Federal Arbitration Act expressly governing**; judgment on the award enforceable in any court. The clause binds the Company and every Member/Participant/Manager; carve-outs: emergency injunctive relief for IP/confidentiality breaches; statutory claims non-waivable by law.

15.2 **Ledger appeals** per §6.4 (arbitrator applies Schedule B only).

15.3 **Deadlock buy-sell.** If a Tier-3 matter necessary to the business fails in two votes across two consecutive quarters, any holder(s) of ≥10% of Ledger Shares may initiate appraisal: an independent appraiser [bracket: dual-appraisal/baseball method] determines fair value without minority or marketability discounts [bracket]; the continuing Participants (pro rata) or the Company purchase the initiating or blocking minority's interests at that value over **[24–36]** months. **No shotgun clause** is included, by design.

15.4 Nothing herein limits any court's authority under M.G.L. c. 156C §44; the parties acknowledge the intent that §15.1–15.3 make resort to §44 unnecessary.

## Article XVI — Compensation Compliance (standalone from equity)

16.1 The Company shall, on counsel's timeline: (a) cease accrual of new wage-law violations by commencing lawful payroll for all non-owner service providers or lawfully suspending their services; (b) implement a counsel-designed remediation of accrued unpaid-wage exposure; (c) thereafter maintain wage-and-hour compliance (minimum wage, timing, records, classification).

16.2 CP accrual for any paid Participant is limited to the §5.2 gap formula. No Participant may be required or permitted to waive wage rights in connection with this Agreement.
