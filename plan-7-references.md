# plan-7 — Annotated Bibliography & Evidence Ratings

**Verification method statement (honesty first).** The research environment's egress proxy returned HTTP 403 policy denials on direct fetches (WebFetch/curl) to effectively all non-GitHub hosts, across all five research agents. Therefore:
- **Fetch-verified** = the page was actually retrieved and read (1 source).
- **Search-verified** = the exact URL was returned by the live web-search index on 2026-08-01/02 together with title and content excerpts matching the claim attributed to it. This confirms the URL exists and hosts matching content, but is weaker than a byte-level fetch. All other sources below are in this category.
- **⚠** = the specific number cited reached us via a secondary source's summary (search snippet quoting the primary), and should be re-checked against the primary before use in a legal or fundraising document.

Evidence-strength scale used: **A** = peer-reviewed causal (experiment/IV) · **B** = peer-reviewed correlational or meta-analytic · **C** = statute / case law / official government or university policy · **D** = large-N proprietary industry data · **E** = practitioner framework, template, or law-firm explainer · **F** = commentary/anecdote.
