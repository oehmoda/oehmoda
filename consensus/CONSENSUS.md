# The Consensus Design — Governance & Equity for the Company

The single unified answer produced from the nine independent plans by the
plurality procedure in `CONSENSUS_PLAN.md`. Every ruling traces to a tally row
in `tally.md` — bracketed footnotes give the vote, e.g. `[Q5: 6/9]`. Rulings
that won with three or fewer supporting plans are flagged **(LC)**. Nothing
here is legal advice; the plans themselves unanimously route execution through
Massachusetts counsel.

---

## 1. Equity architecture

The company adopts a **hybrid model**: a fixed founder reserve earned over
time, plus a dynamic contribution ledger for everything else `[Q1: 8/9]`. On a
cap table normalized to 100%:

- **25% Founder Reserve** — exactly 5% per founder, equal for all five, and a
  ceiling to be *earned*, never a grant `[Q2: 8/9]`.
- **75% Contribution Pool** — allocated continuously by a Slicing-Pie-style
  ledger on identical terms for founders, employees, and the advisor
  `[Q3: 8/8]`. The professor's fixed 1.0% advisor grant is carved from this
  pool (participants share the remaining 74%) `[Coherence re-tally: 4/9]`.

There is no unissued buffer at the LLC stage; the option pool comes later, at
the freeze (§6).

### The ledger formula `[Q3]`

Contribution points accrue monthly:

- **Unpaid time:** verified hours × the published Rate-Card rate for the role
  actually performed × **2.0** (8/8). If partly paid, the gap earns points.
- **Cash invested:** × **4.0** (8/8). Voluntary only — cash earns points, not
  units directly, and no member is ever obligated to contribute capital
  **(LC, appendix)**.
- **Approved property/IP:** appraised FMV × **2.0** (6/6 voting).
- **Unreimbursed expenses:** × **4.0** (3/3 voting) **(LC)**.
- **No quality, discretion, or outcome multiplier** (6/8) — and **no idea or
  vision premium**: leadership, strategy, and fundraising work is priced at
  the standard band rate for that work, never a self-priced founder rate
  `[Q30: 3/3]` **(LC)**.

The Rate Card is Board-adopted from published ~50th-percentile market data
(BLS/Carta-class sources) per role performed, not per title held (8/8). Your
share of the pool = your cumulative points ÷ everyone's cumulative points.

### Year 1 — the retroactive settlement `[Q4: 8/9]`

The undocumented first year is settled **once**, through the *same* formula:
every participant (including inactive founders) submits an evidence-backed
statement (git history, documents, calendars); gaps are filled with coarse
default-hour conventions rather than false precision (3/8) **(LC)**; the
Ledger Committee (§7) adjudicates, publishes the full draft grid to all
members with an objection window, and its resolution is **final and
non-reopenable** (5/9) — no renegotiation later.

### Founder Reserve vesting `[Q5]`

Each founder's 5% earns in **16 equal quarterly tranches over 4 years, no
cliff** (7/9 length; 5/9 quarterly; 6/9 no cliff), **retroactive to founding**
for months a founder can evidence active service (5/9). Every tranche is
**activity-gated**: it vests only if the founder met an objective hours-based
test that quarter — roughly ≥20 hours/week of ledger-verified work (≈250
hours/quarter) (8/9 gate; 4/9 threshold). Performance objectives are *not* a
vesting condition (hours only, 5+/9). A missed tranche is **permanently
forfeited into the Contribution Pool** for all participants (8/9) — nobody's
departure enriches the other founders specifically.

## 2. Inactive founders `[Q8: unanimous core]`

Self-executing economics, no confrontation:

- They **keep every unit they earned** — economics and votes — forever. No
  confiscation, no forced buyout (9/9).
- Unearned reserve tranches **forfeit automatically**, quarter by quarter, into
  the pool (9/9).
- Founder status confers **nothing**: no reserved board seat, office, veto, or
  title rights (9/9).
- There is **no buyout mechanism** (4/9): an inactive founder simply stops
  earning and dilutes naturally as others' points accumulate.

## 3. Employees `[Q7]`

The ~10 employees earn equity through the **same ledger, same formula, same
Rate Card** as founders (8/9). Units are issued as **profits interests** under
Rev. Proc. 93-27/2001-43 (7/9), with a parallel **phantom-unit / unit-
appreciation track** for anyone who needs to stay W-2 rather than become a K-1
partner (4/9). At the freeze, a conventional **12.5% (12–15%) option pool** is
created for future hires (7/9).

## 4. The professor `[Q9]`

The oral "decent share" promise is replaced — not honored vaguely, not
ignored — by a **written advisor agreement** with an integration clause,
conditioned on **Yale conflict-of-interest clearance and IP-boundary terms**
before anything accrues (9/9). His package (4/9 structure):

- A **fixed 1.0% advisor grant** (4/6 of granting plans), vesting **monthly
  over 24 months** (6/6), carved from the Contribution Pool;
- **plus ledger participation** at his documented external expert consulting
  rate (~$350–500/hr band, 7/7) for actual logged hours — the "higher rate he
  deserves," delivered objectively through everyone's formula.

## 5. Freeze Event `[Q6]`

The ledger runs until the **earliest** of:

1. a bona fide **priced financing ≥ $1M** from unaffiliated investors (6/8);
2. a **sale or change of control** (tie-broken: required for the drag-along to
   function);
3. **two consecutive cash-flow-positive quarters** with everyone paid roughly
   market compensation (tie-broken by blind re-vote 2–1: breakeven is the
   natural terminus of an at-risk ledger);
4. an **elective freeze** by Board majority + 66⅔% of units (6/8).

There is **no fixed time backstop** (re-vote 2–1: an arbitrary clock could
freeze percentages while contributions are still at risk; the elective vote is
the escape hatch). At the freeze, percentages crystallize permanently into
fixed units and convert pro rata into Delaware C-corp common stock (8/8), and
the 12.5% option pool is created.

## 6. Entity, tax, and the legal emergency

**Entity** `[Q14]`: stay a **Massachusetts LLC** now (9/9); every member
pre-consents in the OA to a **Delaware C-corp conversion executed at the
Freeze Event** (9/9 pre-consent; 7/9 timing), with a cooperation covenant so no
one can hold it up.

**Tax** `[Q16]`: settle the retroactive Year-1 equity **now, while FMV is
provably nominal**, with a contemporaneous third-party valuation (8/9);
**protective 83(b) elections within 30 days of every grant** (9/9); ongoing
accrual as profits interests (7/9); conversion timing managed so the QSBS
§1202 clock (post-OBBBA regime) starts as early as practical (7/9). Grants are
papered as a written compensatory plan under Rule 701 with the Massachusetts
blue-sky exemption `[Q27: 3/3]` **(LC)**. No SAFEs or convertibles at the LLC
stage **(LC, appendix)**.

**Wage Act — the gating item** `[Q15: 9/9]`: before or alongside signing,
counsel-led remediation of the unpaid-worker exposure (mandatory treble
damages, personal liability for managing founders, no valid waivers): sort all
~15 people into lawful ≥$15/hr payroll or bona fide member status, quantify
the lookback, and paper that **equity supplements wages and never substitutes
for them**. Every plan treats this as non-optional and urgent.

## 7. Governance

**Board** `[Q10]`: manager-managed LLC (9/9) with a **3-seat Board of
Managers** (8/9): the **CEO ex officio** (5/9), **one seat elected annually**
by members voting in proportion to units (8/9 annual; 6/9 unit-weighted), and
**one independent seat** — no material ties to any founder (4/9).

**CEO** `[Q11]`: **exactly one** (9/9 — co-CEO structures explicitly
rejected), appointed by the Board (8/9), **removable at any time by majority
of the non-CEO Managers** (8/9; abstention mechanics 3/9 **LC**). Losing the
office never touches earned equity or member status **(LC)**. Founders hold
written role charters with quarterly objectives reviewed by the CEO/Board
`[Q25: 2/2]` **(LC)**; day-to-day authority sits with named DRIs per function
`[Q26: 2/2]` **(LC)** — and neither objectives nor performance ever gate
equity, which is a function of verified hours only.

**Decision tiers** `[Q12]`: ordinary course → CEO; significant matters →
Board majority; a defined **reserved-matter list → Board majority + 66⅔% of
voting units** (8/9). **No unanimity requirement anywhere** in ongoing
governance (7/9) — the one unanimous act is the initial signing of the
restated OA itself (3/9 uncontradicted). One class of voting units,
one-unit-one-vote, no founder class or super-voting `[Q23: 3/4]` **(LC)**.
Default c.156C fiduciary duties are retained, not contractually tailored
`[Q24: 2/3]` **(LC)**.

**Ledger administration** `[Q21: 5/5]`: a standing **Ledger Committee**,
elected annually by members, founder-minority with at least one non-founder,
deliberately separate from the Board, with verification-only powers against
the published formula. The complete ledger and cap table are published to all
members **at least quarterly** `[Q22: 4/5]`. Knowingly inflated entries
forfeit 3× the inflated points **(LC, appendix)**.

## 8. Disputes `[Q13]`

Time-boxed ladder (8/9): direct negotiation (10–15 days) → JAMS/AAA mediation
in Boston (30 days) → **binding single-arbitrator arbitration**, Boston,
Massachusetts law. Ledger and valuation disputes get a fast-track
**final-offer ("baseball") expert determination** (4/9). **No shotgun
clause** (6/9). A reserved-matter deadlock persisting ~60 days resolves by an
**appraised fair-value buyout** with no minority or marketability discounts
(4/9).

## 9. Papering and covenants

- **Supersession** `[Q20: 5/5]`: the restated OA supersedes every prior
  informal understanding — the original handshake, later reallocations, and
  the professor's oral promise — with signed acknowledgments from everyone.
- **IP** `[Q18: 6/6]`: present-assignment IP and confidentiality agreements
  from every past and present contributor, retroactive to inception, as a
  condition of receiving any equity.
- **No noncompetes** `[Q17: 4/5]` — confidentiality, IP assignment, and a
  12-month non-solicit only (MA Noncompetition Agreement Act makes more
  largely unenforceable here anyway).
- **Transfers** `[Q19: 5/5]`: locked — Board consent (estate-planning
  exception), company-then-member ROFR, tag-along, and a **drag-along** on any
  Board + 66⅔%-approved sale.
- **Investor readiness**: members pre-consent to the standard first-round
  package including possible re-vesting `[Q28: 2/2]` **(LC)**, and the
  bespoke governance articles **sunset automatically at financing** into
  NVCA-standard structures `[Q29: 2/2]` **(LC)**.

## 10. Execution order

Synthesizing the plans' unanimous sequencing: (1) Wage Act remediation with
counsel — immediately, before signatures; (2) contemporaneous valuation;
(3) unanimous signing of the restated OA + role charters + IP agreements + the
professor's advisor agreement in the same window; (4) Year-1 retroactive
settlement through the committee; (5) 83(b) filings within each 30-day window;
(6) run the ledger quarterly until a Freeze Event; (7) freeze, convert to a
Delaware C-corp, create the 12.5% option pool, adopt NVCA-standard governance.

## Appendix — single-plan provisions (adopted 1/1 by the no-threshold rule; lowest confidence)

- **MOU-first sequencing** (plan-1): ratify the *rules* in a short signed MOU
  before anyone computes their own backfill numbers.
- **Cash recovery floor** (plan-1): documented out-of-pocket cash is always
  recoverable at face value before any forfeiture applies, even for a bad
  leaver.
- **Leadership-ownership floor** (plan-1): if at freeze the CEO + top two
  executives would hold under ~30% combined, the Board rebalances with top-up
  grants from the new option pool.
- **Anti-gaming penalty** (plan-5b): knowingly inflated ledger entries forfeit
  3× the inflated points; repetition is Cause.
- **No mandatory capital calls** (plan-5b): cash is voluntary and earns
  points, never units directly.
- **No SAFEs at the LLC stage** (plan-9): convertibles only after the Delaware
  conversion.

  earn-only reserve; the 2×/4× formula on identical terms; keep-earned/

  CEO-ex-officio + independent board seats; no inactive-founder buyout;
  baseball fast-track; no shotgun; appraised-value deadlock buyout; freeze at
  ≥$1M financing + elective vote; no noncompetes; ledger committee;
  transparency; transfer stack.
- **Thin (≤3, LC):** default-hour conventions for Year-1 gaps; expenses ×4;

  removal-abstention mechanics; everything in the Appendix.
- **Tie-broken:** sale/CoC freeze trigger (compatibility); breakeven trigger
