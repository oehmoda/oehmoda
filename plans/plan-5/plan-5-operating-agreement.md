## Article I — Formation, Purpose, Definitions

1.1 **Continuation.** The Company continues as a Massachusetts limited liability company under M.G.L. c. 156C. This Agreement amends and restates all prior oral or informal arrangements, which are void and superseded upon effectiveness. **[C-1: confirm supersession language adequately extinguishes claims based on the founders' prior informal agreements, including the "reserved allocation" understanding and any third-party promises; see Art. XI for the advisor release.]**

1.2 **Purpose.** [F-1: business purpose] and any lawful act under c. 156C.

1.3 **Key definitions** (business meanings; counsel to formalize):
- **Units:** a single class of membership interests; one vote per Unit. No supervoting class.
- **Ledger:** the Contribution Ledger maintained under Article VII.
- **Points:** contribution units computed under §7.3.
- **Activity Gate, Rate Table, Kicker, RiskMult:** as defined in §§6.2, 7.3.
- **Crystallization Event:** the earlier of (a) closing of a priced equity financing of ≥ $1,000,000, or (b) the Conversion (Art. X) if the Board so elects.
- **Fair Market Value (FMV):** value determined by an independent appraiser selected by the Board (post-Conversion, the then-current 409A valuation). **[C-2: appraisal procedure and dispute mechanics.]**

## Article II — Members; Capital; No Employment

2.1 **Members.** The five founders and the ten current contributors are admitted as Members, each executing this Agreement, an IP assignment (Art. IX), and a confidentiality agreement. Schedule A lists Members and Units.

2.2 **Member status, not employment.** Members participate as owners. No Member is entitled to wages or salary unless separately employed under a written agreement approved by the Board. **[C-3 — CRITICAL: structure Member admission so each of the ten current contributors is a bona fide member/owner and not an "employee" under the Massachusetts Wage Act (M.G.L. c. 149 §148) and the independent-contractor ABC test (c. 149 §148B); assess and remediate any accrued Year-1 wage exposure, including personal exposure of managing officers, before signing. Equity-for-wages "special contracts" are unenforceable; do not paper this as deferred wages.]**

2.3 **Capital contributions.** No Member is obligated to contribute capital except under a Board-approved capital call accepted in writing. Only accepted capital-call cash earns the 4× multiplier (§7.3).

2.4 **Securities compliance.** **[C-4: exemption strategy for Unit issuances to ~15+ persons (§4(a)(2)/Reg D/Rule 506(b)), blue-sky filings, and investment representations; Rule 701 becomes available only post-Conversion.]**

## Article III — Equity Structure

3.1 **Pools.** Authorized Units are allocated:
- (a) **Founder Reserved Pool — 25.0%** (5.0% cap per founder), governed by Article VI;
- (b) **Advisor Pool — 1.0%**, governed by Article XI;
- (c) **Contribution Pool — 74.0%**, governed by Article VII.

3.2 **Single class.** All issued Units are identical in voting and economic rights. No Units may be issued outside the three pools except by Supermajority Consent (§5.4).

3.3 **Unissued/reverted equity.** Cancelled reserved tranches (§6.3) and any unallocated Advisor Pool revert to the Contribution Pool.

## Article IV — Management: Board and Officers

4.1 **Board of Managers.** The Company is manager-managed by a Board of three (3): the CEO ex officio, plus two Managers elected annually by plurality of Units. Any Member may stand for election regardless of founder status. Board acts by majority. At the first priced financing the Board expands to five: one investor designee and one independent approved by mutual consent of the Board and lead investor.

4.2 **CEO.** The Board appoints a Chief Executive Officer by majority following a candidacy process open to all Members; the mandate renews annually by Board vote; the Board may remove the CEO at any time by majority vote. The CEO manages day-to-day operations, executes the budget, and hires/terminates non-officer personnel within budget.

4.3 **Confidence vote.** Members holding ≥ 40% of Units may require a special Board meeting to review the CEO's mandate; the Board's decision stands for at least two quarters absent cause.

4.4 **Contribution Committee.** Three Members elected annually by Unit vote, at least one of whom is not a founder. Administers the Ledger (Art. VII), certifies rate-band assignments, applies Kickers with written justification, and publishes quarterly reports to all Members. A Committee member is recused from any determination affecting their own Points or a related person's.

## Article V — Decision Rights

5.1 **Decision Rights Matrix.** The Board adopts annually a written matrix substantially as follows:

| Decision | Owner |
|---|---|
| Product/technical decisions within a domain and budget | Domain lead (appointed by CEO with Board consent) |
| Cross-domain conflicts; org design; non-officer hiring/firing | CEO |
| Budget; officers; spend > $25,000 off-budget; committee slates; litigation | Board majority |
| Reserved Matters | Supermajority of Units (§5.4) |

5.2 **Records.** Decisions at Board level and above are minuted and available to all Members.

5.3 **Member votes.** One Unit, one vote; ordinary matters by majority of Units present at a noticed meeting or by written consent. **[C-5: notice, quorum, e-consent mechanics.]**

5.4 **Reserved Matters — Supermajority (66⅔% of all Units):** (a) amendment of this Agreement; (b) issuance of equity outside Art. III pools; (c) changes to the Rate Table, multipliers, Activity Gate, or pool sizes; (d) merger, sale of substantially all assets, or dissolution; (e) related-party transactions > $10,000; (f) admission of Members outside Art. VII; (g) variation of the pre-authorized Conversion terms (Art. X); (h) incurring debt > $100,000.

## Article VI — Founder Reserved Pool

6.1 **Grant.** Each founder listed on Schedule B holds a conditional right to up to 5.0% of fully-diluted Units, divided into sixteen (16) equal quarterly tranches of 0.3125%, measured from [F-2: formation date].

6.2 **Activity Gate.** A tranche is earned only if, in that quarter, the founder either (a) recorded ≥ 390 verified contribution-hours in the Ledger, or (b) earned Points ≥ 75% of the median Points of the five highest-Point contributors that quarter.

6.3 **Use-it-or-lose-it.** An unearned tranche is cancelled automatically and permanently at quarter-end and reverts to the Contribution Pool. No carry-forward, no cure, no acceleration, except Board-approved medical/family leave of up to two quarters, which tolls (extends) rather than forgives the schedule. **[C-6: leave-tolling terms.]**

6.4 **Past quarters.** The four quarters preceding effectiveness are resolved in the Reckoning (Art. VIII) against the same Gate using the reconstructed record.

6.5 **Departure.** A founder who resigns, is removed for Cause **[C-7: define Cause]**, or fails the Gate for four consecutive quarters forfeits all future tranches. Earned tranches and earned Points are never forfeited.

## Article VII — Contribution Pool and Ledger

7.1 **Identical terms.** All natural-person contributors — founders, employees-turned-Members, and Track-B advisors — participate in the Contribution Pool under identical rules. No rate, multiplier, cap, or verification standard may distinguish a person by founder or non-founder status.

7.2 **Cycle.** Contributors submit verified logs monthly; the Committee certifies Points quarterly; Contribution Pool interests re-balance quarterly per §7.5 until the Crystallization Event.

7.3 **Points.**
```
points(p,q) = [ Σ_categories verified_hours × Rate(category) ] × RiskMult
              + accepted_capital_call_cash × 4.0
              + approved_out_of_pocket_expenses × 2.0
              all × Kicker(p,q) ∈ [0.75, 1.25]
RiskMult = 2.0 while p receives no cash compensation; 1.0 in any month p draws ≥ 50% of Rate in cash
```
- **Rate Table (Schedule C, initial):** software engineering $64/hr; senior/staff engineering & architecture $80/hr; engineering management / business planning / technical vision $80/hr; specialized ML research ≤ $128/hr via §7.4; admin/ops $30/hr. Anchored to BLS OEWS medians (÷2,080); re-benchmarked annually by the Committee; changes are a Reserved Matter.
- **Caps:** 60 loggable hours/person/week. Kicker requires written justification, recusal, and publication.
- Rates attach to the category of work performed, not to title or status.

7.4 **Premium-rate petition.** Any contributor may petition for a rate above the Table by submitting two independent written FMV benchmarks for comparable services; Committee approval required; hard cap of 2.0× the base engineering rate. Status (professorship, founder title, seniority) is not a benchmark.

7.5 **Rebalancing.** Each quarter, each contributor's share of the Contribution Pool equals lifetime certified Points ÷ all lifetime certified Points, implemented by issuance/adjustment of Units. **[C-8: implement quarterly rebalancing in a tax-efficient manner — e.g., escalating profits-interest grants under Rev. Proc. 93-27/2001-43 versus periodic capital-interest true-ups; advise on tax reporting for Members.]**

7.6 **Crystallization.** Upon the Crystallization Event the Ledger closes permanently: Contribution Pool percentages freeze at last-certified values, convert per Art. X, and future compensation moves to salaries and a new option plan (target pool 10–15% post-money, per then-current market data). Certified Points earned before closure are fully earned.

7.7 **Disputes.** Ledger disputes go to the Committee, then to Art. XII. Certified quarters more than two quarters old may not be reopened absent fraud.

## Article VIII — Retrospective Reckoning; Investor Re-Vesting

8.1 **Reckoning.** Within 60 days of effectiveness, the Committee applies §7.3 to the period from formation to effectiveness: contributors submit reconstructed logs with documentary evidence and two peer attestations; documented hours count 100%, attested-undocumented 75%, contested 50% pending resolution; 60 hr/week retroactive cap; RiskMult 2.0 throughout. Output is ratified by Supermajority and issued within 30 days. **[C-9: valuation support and 83(b) mechanics for the retroactive issuance while FMV is low.]**

8.2 **Pre-committed re-vesting.** Each Member agrees in advance that, if required by the lead investor of the first priced financing, up to 25% of their then-held equity may be made subject to service-based re-vesting over up to 24 months, on terms applied uniformly to all Members.

## Article IX — IP; Restrictive Covenants

9.1 **IP assignment.** Every Member and contributor executes a present-tense assignment of all Company-related work product; prior-year work product is assigned in the Reckoning submissions. **[C-10: confirm chain of title on all Year-1 work by then-unpaid contributors.]**

9.2 **Confidentiality; non-solicit.** Perpetual confidentiality; 12-month employee/customer non-solicit post-departure.

9.3 **No default noncompete.** No noncompete is imposed on Members as such. **[C-11: advise whether M.G.L. c. 149 §24L (garden-leave/consideration requirements, 12-month cap) applies to Members; whether member-status or sale-of-business noncompetes are worth pursuing for key persons; do not draft anything unenforceable under §24L.]**

## Article X — Conversion to Delaware C-Corporation

10.1 **Pre-authorization.** The Board is authorized, without further Member vote, to effect a statutory conversion/merger of the Company into a Delaware corporation on these terms: Units convert pro rata into a single class of common stock; unearned reserved tranches convert into restricted stock with the identical earn-in Gate; the Ledger (if not yet crystallized) continues to operate on reserved share pools; Members deliver 83(b) elections within 30 days of any restricted grant. Material deviations are a Reserved Matter.

10.2 **Timing intent.** The Members intend Conversion promptly after the Reckoning to begin the §1202 QSBS holding period at low valuation. **[C-12: QSBS qualification at Conversion — gross-asset test, active-business test, redemption look-back interaction with the §13.2 call option; sequence redemptions to avoid tainting QSBS.]**

10.3 **Post-conversion plan.** Adopt an equity incentive plan under Rule 701 with 409A valuations. **[C-13.]**

## Article XI — Advisors; the Promised Advisor

11.1 **Advisor Pool.** Up to 1.0% fully diluted; standard grants follow FAST-style benchmarks (0.15–1.0% by stage and engagement), 24-month monthly vesting, 3-month cliff, Board-approved.

11.2 **The [F-3: professor] grant.** Conditioned on ALL of: (a) execution of an advisor agreement including a full release of any claim arising from prior informal promises of equity; (b) written confirmation of compliance with Yale COI policy (annual disclosure, one-day-per-week external-activity limit) and Yale review of the equity interest where required; (c) representations that no Yale resources, personnel, facilities, or funding were or will be used and that no contributed IP is subject to Yale's patent policy, plus a present assignment of all contributions; (d) selection of **Track A** (0.5% Advisor Pool grant, uplift to 0.8% upon a Board-approved commitment of ≥ 10 hrs/month with deliverables) **or Track B** (participation in the Contribution Pool under Art. VII at a §7.4-benchmarked rate). **[C-14: draft the release; evaluate promissory-estoppel exposure on the oral promise; confirm no Yale-claimable IP is already embedded in the Company's product before any grant issues.]**

## Article XII — Dispute Resolution; Deadlock; Transfers

12.1 **Ladder.** (a) written notice + 14-day good-faith negotiation; (b) 30-day mediation (AAA/JAMS, Boston); (c) binding arbitration, AAA Commercial Rules, seat Boston, one arbitrator, fee-shifting available for bad-faith claims. **[C-15: carve-outs for injunctive IP/confidentiality relief and non-arbitrable statutory claims (e.g., Wage Act).]**

12.2 **Deadlock.** If a Reserved Matter or Board matter remains blocked > 90 days after mediation, either bloc (≥ 40% of Units) may trigger a **sealed-bid auction**: each bloc submits a sealed per-Unit price; the higher bidder purchases the other bloc's Units at the higher price, closing within 90 days with up to 36-month seller financing at [F-4]% interest. A shotgun/Texas-shootout mechanism is deliberately NOT used. **[C-16: enforceability, financing terms, and an appraisal-based put/call fallback.]**

12.3 **Transfers.** No transfer of Units without Board consent, except estate-planning transfers to controlled entities. Company right of first refusal; tag-along on founder block sales ≥ 10%; drag-along at Supermajority plus Board approval.

12.4 **Departure call option.** On any Member's departure (resignation, removal, four consecutive failed Gates for founders, or cessation of contribution for 12 months), the Company may, within 180 days, purchase any or all of that Member's Units at FMV, payable over up to 36 months. Earned/vested equity is never repurchased below FMV; nothing herein forfeits earned equity.

## Article XIII — Tax, Books, Miscellaneous

13.1 **Tax.** Partnership taxation until Conversion; partnership representative appointed by the Board. **[C-17: tax distributions to cover Members' pass-through liabilities; K-1 logistics for ~15 Members.]**

13.2 **Books; transparency.** Ledger reports, Rate Table, minutes, and cap table are available to all Members quarterly.

13.3 **Amendment.** Reserved Matter (66⅔%), except Schedule updates ministerially reflecting Ledger operation, which the Committee certifies.

13.4 **Governing law.** Massachusetts (internal affairs) until Conversion; Delaware thereafter. **[C-18: dispute-forum coordination across the Conversion boundary.]**
