## Article I — Formation, Purpose, Definitions

**1.1 Continuation.** The Company was formed under the Massachusetts Limited Liability
Company Act, M.G.L. c. 156C (the "Act"), on [DATE ≈ one year ago] (the "Formation
Date"). This Agreement amends and restates all prior oral or informal understandings,
which are expressly superseded (see 14.9).

**1.2 Purpose.** Any lawful business, presently the development and commercialization
of [SOFTWARE/ML PRODUCT].

**1.3 Principal office.** [ADDRESS], Massachusetts. `[⚖ Confirm registered agent and
annual report status with the Secretary of the Commonwealth.]`

**1.4 Key definitions** (full list for counsel to expand):

- **"Member"** — any holder of Units admitted under this Agreement. Founders,
  employees who hold Units, and the Advisor (Article VIII) are all Members with
  identical Unit rights; there are no founder-class Units.
- **"Units"** — a single class of common membership interests, plus **"Incentive
  Units"** structured as profits interests (see 3.6).
- **"Contribution Points" / "Points"** — the unit of account of the Contribution
  Ledger (Article IV).
- **"Contribution Percentage"** — a person's Points ÷ all outstanding Points, as
  recomputed each quarter under 4.6.
- **"Founder"** — the five individuals listed on Schedule A: [F1, F2, F3, F4, F5].
  "Founder" is a historical label only; it confers no ongoing rights except the
  Founder Reserve of Article V.
- **"Active Service"** — in any calendar quarter, either (a) ≥ 240 Accepted Hours
  (Article IV) in that quarter, or (b) Accepted Points ≥ 50% of the median Accepted
  Points of all Full-Time Participants that quarter, whichever is lower. (Objective
  test; no discretionary findings of "activity.")
- **"Freeze Event"** — earliest of: (i) closing of Qualified Financing (cumulative
  new equity or SAFE-equivalent investment ≥ $2,000,000); (ii) three consecutive
  months of positive operating cash flow; (iii) a Change of Control; (iv) election by
  Supermajority Member Vote (66⅔% of Units) plus Board approval.
- **"Supermajority Member Vote"** — 66⅔% of then-outstanding Units (post-freeze) or
  of then-current Contribution Percentages plus earned Founder Reserve (pre-freeze).

## Article II — Members, Units, No Founder Class

**2.1 Single equity ladder.** All equity in the Company is issued and earned through
exactly two channels, and no others:

1. the **Founder Reserve** (Article V): a hard-capped 25.0% (5.0% per Founder),
   earned quarter-by-quarter, forfeitable ("use it or lose it"); and
2. the **Contribution Pool** (Article IV): 75.0% of the Company, plus any forfeited
   Founder Reserve tranches, allocated purely by the Contribution Ledger, on
   **identical terms for Founders, employees, and the Advisor**.

**2.2 No other issuances.** No Units may be issued outside 2.1 except (a) to
investors in a bona fide financing approved under Article X, or (b) by Supermajority
Member Vote plus Board approval.

**2.3 Admission of employees as Members.** Each service provider who accrues Points
executes a joinder, the Contribution & IP Assignment Agreement (11.2), and a spousal
consent if applicable `[⚖ confirm need under MA law]`, and becomes a Member upon first
allocation. `[⚖ Securities compliance for each grant — Rule 701 analog for LLC
interests / Reg D 506(b); see 11.5.]`

**2.4 Voting.** Pre-Freeze, each Member votes their current Contribution Percentage
plus earned Founder Reserve, as of the most recent quarterly Statement (4.6).
Post-Freeze, one Unit = one vote.

**2.5 Transfer restrictions.** No transfers except to the Company, with Board consent,
or estate-planning transfers to revocable trusts; right of first refusal to the
Company then Members pro rata; drag-along at Change of Control approved by
Supermajority Member Vote; tag-along on Founder secondary sales. `[⚖ standard ROFR /
drag / tag drafting].`

## Article III — Capital, Tax, and the Conversion Path

**3.1 No mandatory capital calls.** No Member is obligated to contribute capital.
Voluntary cash contributions earn Points under 4.4(b) — they do not buy Units directly.

**3.2 Tax classification.** Partnership taxation while an LLC. Members receive K-1s;
the Company will make quarterly **tax distributions** to cover Members' pass-through
tax on allocated income (if any) at an assumed combined rate of [40]%, before any
other distributions. `[⚖ drafting of allocations, capital accounts, 704(b) compliance.]`

**3.3 Planned conversion.** Upon the earlier of (a) a Freeze Event triggered by a
Qualified Financing, or (b) Board determination that a financing on corporate stock
(including SAFEs, which are drafted for corporations) is imminent, the Company **shall**
convert by statutory conversion into a **Delaware C-corporation**, with each Member's
frozen percentage exchanged for common stock of the same percentage (subject to 3.5).
Each Member pre-consents to this conversion now; no further member vote is required
if percentages are preserved. `[⚖ conversion mechanics: DGCL §265 + M.G.L. c.156C;
IRC §351 / Rev. Rul. 84-111 method selection; QSBS timing analysis — converting
earlier starts the §1202 five-year clock and (post-2025-Act) tiered exclusion at a low
basis, but a later conversion raises the 10×-basis cap; counsel + tax advisor to model
both. Note gross-asset limits ($75M for post-July-4-2025 stock).]`

**3.4 Post-conversion pool.** The conversion shall create an employee option pool of
[12]% post-conversion, and standard NVCA-form documents shall be used for the
financing. Rotating/dynamic mechanisms in this Agreement **sunset at conversion**
(Article XIII).

**3.5 Reverse vesting at financing.** Members acknowledge investors will likely
require founders and key holders to re-subject a portion of already-earned stock to
time-based reverse vesting at the priced round; each Founder pre-agrees to re-vesting
of up to [25]% of their then-holdings over [2] years if the lead investor requires it.

**3.6 Incentive Units as profits interests.** All equity earned through the
Contribution Ledger after the Restatement Date is issued as Incentive Units intended
to qualify as **profits interests** (Rev. Proc. 93-27 / 2001-43 safe harbors), each
tranche with a distribution threshold equal to Company value at grant, so grants are
not taxable compensation at issuance and require no 409A appraisal. Members shall file
**protective 83(b) elections within 30 days** of each grant. Retroactive Year-1
allocations under 4.8: `[⚖ OPEN — issuing year-1 equity as capital interests at
today's (still-low) value vs. catch-up profits interests with a nominal threshold;
counsel/tax to choose the structure that minimizes ordinary income while surviving
diligence. Do NOT close this without a valuation memo.]`

## Article IV — The Contribution Pool (75% + recycled Reserve)

*The intent of this Article: a spreadsheet, not a negotiation. Any person's share
must be computable by a third party from the Ledger alone.*

**4.1 Identical terms.** Founders, employees, and the Advisor accrue Points under the
same formulas, the same Rate Table, and the same multipliers. No person has a
negotiated rate, premium, or discount. (The Advisor's higher market rate arises from
the Rate Table itself, not from a special deal — see Article VIII.)

**4.2 The Contribution Ledger.** A single auditable ledger (spreadsheet or equivalent
tool) recording, per person per quarter: Accepted Hours by role level, cash in,
unreimbursed expenses, and contributed property. Maintained by the Allocation
Committee (Article VII). Entries must be logged within **14 days** of the contribution
and accepted or challenged within **14 days** thereafter; unchallenged entries are final.

**4.3 Rate Table.** A published table of hourly rates by role level, set as the
**median US market total-cash compensation** for that level from [Carta Total Comp /
levels.fyi / Pave — pick two and average] ÷ 2,080 hours, refreshed each January by
the Allocation Committee and ratified by the Board. Initial table (to be confirmed
against current benchmark data at signing):

| Level | Description | Annual benchmark | Hourly rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| E3 | Software engineer | $[150,000] | $[72] |
| E4 | Senior engineer | $[195,000] | $[94] |
| E5 | Staff engineer / eng. lead | $[240,000] | $[115] |
| M | Engineering management (per hour actually managing) | $[220,000] | $[106] |
| X | External specialist/advisor (e.g., ML professor) | **documented consulting FMV, capped at $[450]/hr** | — |
| B | Business planning / vision / fundraising execution | rate of the person's engineering level | — |

*Vision and strategy work is compensated as hours at the contributor's level rate —
there is no "idea premium." Level assignments are made by the Allocation Committee
against the published leveling rubric and may be appealed under Article XII.*

**4.4 Point formulas.** For each person *i* in quarter *q*:

- (a) **Unpaid time:** `Points = AcceptedHours × Rate(level) × 2.0`
- (b) **Cash invested:** `Points = Cash × 4.0`
- (c) **Unreimbursed approved expenses:** `Points = Amount × 4.0`
- (d) **Contributed property/pre-existing IP** (appraised FMV, Board-approved):
  `Points = FMV × 2.0`
- (e) **Partially paid time (once salaries begin):**
  `Points = max(0, Rate(level) × AcceptedHours − CashComp) × 2.0`
- (f) Nothing else generates Points. Titles, seniority, tenure, and the "Founder"
  label generate no Points.

The 2×/4× multipliers compensate risk (unpaid labor and cash at total risk), following
the Slicing Pie convention; they may be amended only by Supermajority Member Vote.

**4.5 Accepted Hours.** Hours count only if (i) logged per 4.2, (ii) tied to a
deliverable, ticket, or OKR under a DRI's area (Article VI), and (iii) accepted by
that DRI (or by the Allocation Committee where the contributor *is* the DRI).
Acceptance is of *the work's existence and good faith*, not a performance grade;
rejection requires a written reason and is appealable (Article XII). A person's
self-managed hours cap: no more than [50] hours/week are creditable.

**4.6 Quarterly statement & allocation.** Within 21 days after quarter end, the
Allocation Committee publishes to all Members: each person's quarterly Points, running
lifetime Points, and resulting Contribution Percentage:

`ContributionPct(i) = LifetimePoints(i) ÷ Σ LifetimePoints × (75% + RecycledReserve%)`

**4.7 Dynamic until Freeze.** Percentages float with the Ledger until the Freeze
Event, at which moment each person's Contribution Percentage and earned Founder
Reserve convert to fixed Units, and the Ledger closes. Post-Freeze incentives come
from the option pool (3.4).

**4.8 Retroactive Year 1 (one-time true-up).** Within 60 days of the Restatement
Date, the Ledger is seeded for the period Formation Date → Restatement Date:

1. Each person submits a reconstruction of hours from contemporaneous artifacts
   (commits, PRs, docs, calendars, email) mapped to quarters.
2. Where records are thin, defaults apply: a person shown by artifacts to have worked
   substantially full-time is credited [45] h/week for documented full-time months;
   substantially part-time, [15] h/week; dormant months, 0.
3. Every submission is peer-attested by two non-conflicted participants and accepted
   by the Allocation Committee; disputes go straight to expedited resolution
   (Article XII) — the true-up may not be held open past 120 days.
4. Year-1 cash and expenses enter at 4.4(b)–(c) with documentation.
5. The same Rate Table and multipliers apply to founders and employees alike.

**4.9 Anti-gaming.** Logged hours are certified under penalty of forfeiture: a Member
who knowingly inflates entries forfeits 3× the inflated Points (Committee finding,
appealable). Repeated violation is Cause under 9.4.

## Article V — Founder Reserve (25%): earned, never granted

**5.1 Structure.** Each Founder has a personal reserve of **5.0%**, divided into
**16 equal quarterly tranches of 0.3125%** across the 16 quarters following the
Formation Date.

**5.2 Earning test.** A Founder earns a given quarter's tranche **only if** they met
the Active Service definition (1.4) in that quarter. There is no partial credit, no
make-up quarters, and no Board discretion to waive the test.

**5.3 Retroactive application.** The Year-1 true-up (4.8) determines Active Service
for the four elapsed quarters from the same evidence. A Founder inactive in a past
quarter has already lost that tranche.

**5.4 Forfeiture and recycling.** Unearned tranches are cancelled at quarter end and
their percentage is added to the Contribution Pool ("Recycled Reserve"), where it is
competed for by everyone under Article IV. If a Founder ceases service entirely,
all their future tranches recycle immediately.

**5.5 No acceleration** except: (a) on a Change of Control before the 16 quarters
elapse, tranches for the remaining quarters are earned only by Founders in Active
Service in the two quarters preceding closing (partial single-trigger); (b) death or
permanent disability of an Active-Service Founder earns the next [4] tranches.
`[⚖ confirm treatment; pure design choice flagged for the founders.]`

**5.6 The label ends here.** Beyond this Article, Founders have no rights, vetoes,
board seats, or classes not available to any Member.

## Article VI — Management: Board of Managers, CEO, DRIs

**6.1 Manager-managed.** The Company is manager-managed under M.G.L. c. 156C. Members
acting as members have no agency authority.

**6.2 Board of Managers.** Three (3) Managers. All three are **elected annually** by
Member vote (voting power per 2.4); any Member in Active Service is eligible —
Founder status is neither necessary nor sufficient. Vacancies filled by Member vote
within 30 days. A Manager may be removed at any time with or without cause by
Members holding 60% of voting power. `[⚖ whether Managers owe default 156C fiduciary
duties or modified duties; do not exculpate the duty of loyalty for the Allocation
Committee interface.]`

**6.3 CEO.** The Board appoints a single accountable **CEO** for a renewable
**12-month term**, by majority Board vote, from any Member (or an external hire).
The CEO may be removed by the Board at any time. The CEO is *ex officio* invited to
Board meetings; if the CEO is also an elected Manager, the Board must include at
least one non-executive Manager. Non-renewal is not a penalty and triggers no equity
consequence — office and equity are fully decoupled.

**6.4 DRIs (Directly Responsible Individuals).** The CEO designates, and the Board
ratifies, a DRI for each standing area (initially: Engineering, Product, Research/ML,
Operations/Finance, People). DRIs (a) own day-to-day decisions in their area and
(b) accept contribution entries in their area (4.5). DRI assignments are reviewed at
least annually and published.

**6.5 Decision matrix (the "two-door" rule).**

| Class | Examples | Who decides | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reversible, in-area ("two-way door") | tooling, design choices, sprint scope, publishing a blog post | DRI | Decide fast; consult affected DRIs; disagree-and-commit applies |
| Cross-area or costly-reversible | roadmap changes, comp policy, Rate Table ratification, hiring/termination, budget, spending > $[10,000], litigation, declaring a Freeze/conversion trigger under 3.3(b) | Board (majority) | Written proposal + 48h comment window unless urgent |
| Irreversible / fundamental ("one-way door") | amendment of Articles II, IV, V, this row; sale/merger; dissolution; new Units outside 2.1; changing multipliers or the Rate Table *methodology*; admitting an investor on non-NVCA-standard control terms | **Supermajority Member Vote (66⅔%) + Board majority** | Two meetings ≥ 7 days apart |

**6.6 Disagree-and-commit.** A Member who loses a two-way-door decision may escalate
once, in writing, to the Board; after the Board rules, non-sabotage commitment is a
duty of all Members.

**6.7 No compensation for office.** Manager and CEO roles carry no equity or Points
by virtue of the office; the hours spent are logged like any other hours (level M or
B rates).

## Article VII — Allocation Committee

**7.1 Composition.** Three Members elected annually by Member vote; **at most one may
be a Founder**, and no two Committee members may be Managers. Serving on the
Committee is logged as level-B hours.

**7.2 Duties.** Maintain the Ledger; publish quarterly Statements (4.6); set the Rate
Table annually (Board ratifies); run the Year-1 true-up; make level assignments;
adjudicate acceptance disputes at first instance.

**7.3 Conflicts.** A Committee member is recused from any entry concerning themselves
or a household/family member; the Board appoints an alternate for that item.

**7.4 Audit right.** Any Member may inspect the full Ledger at any time. Once per
year, Members holding 10% may require an external review of the Ledger at Company
expense.

**8.3 Cap and floor.** The Advisor's aggregate equity is capped at **2.0%** at Freeze

## Article IX — Departures, Inactivity, and Dead-Equity Hygiene

**9.1 Earned is kept (default).** Points and earned Reserve tranches represent
delivered value; a person who stops contributing keeps what they earned to date but
accrues nothing further. Their percentage naturally dilutes as others keep earning.

**9.2 Inactivity call option.** If a Member records **zero Accepted Points for four
consecutive quarters** pre-Freeze, the Company holds a call option on all of that
Member's equity at **appraised fair market value**, exercisable by Board decision,
payable [25]% at closing and the rest over 36 months with interest at the AFR.
Valuation disputes go to baseball arbitration (12.4).

**9.3 Voluntary exit.** Same as 9.1, plus the 9.2 call becomes exercisable
immediately at FMV.

**9.4 Cause.** On termination for Cause (fraud, material IP breach, conviction of a
felony involving the Company, deliberate Ledger fraud under 4.9), the Member forfeits
(a) all unearned Reserve and (b) Points accrued in the trailing 12 months, and the
Company may buy remaining equity at the **lower of FMV or $100**. `[⚖ enforceability
of forfeiture-for-cause under MA law; draft Cause narrowly and procedurally.]`

**9.5 Death/disability.** Estate keeps earned equity; Company has a ROFR at FMV;
Company shall seek key-person insurance for the CEO and DRIs when funded.

**9.6 Pre-financing cleanup.** The Board is authorized (and directed) to use 9.2 to
clear wholly inactive holders from the cap table before a priced round, subject to
the FMV and payment-terms protections above.

## Article X — Financings and Investor Protections

**10.1 Authorization.** The Board negotiates financings; any priced round or
instrument > $[250,000] requires Board approval; terms outside NVCA-standard economics
and control require Supermajority Member Vote.

**10.2 Pre-agreed investor accommodations.** Members now pre-consent to, at the first
qualified priced round: Delaware C-corp conversion (3.3), option-pool creation (3.4),
customary reverse vesting (3.5), 1× non-participating preferred liquidation
preference, pro-rata rights, and standard information rights — so that no member
renegotiation can hold up a financing.

**10.3 No pay-to-play among Members.** Members are never obligated to invest to
avoid punitive dilution beyond ordinary pro-rata mathematics.

## Article XII — Disputes and Deadlock

**12.1 Ladder.** (1) Direct written negotiation (10 days) → (2) mediation, JAMS/AAA
Boston (30 days) → (3) final, binding arbitration, single arbitrator, AAA Commercial
Expedited, seat Boston, MA law. No court litigation except to enforce awards or seek
interim IP relief.

**12.2 Ledger disputes (fast track).** Acceptance/level/true-up appeals: Allocation
Committee (14 days) → single-arbitrator **documents-only** arbitration within 45
days; the Ledger is provisionally applied meanwhile.

**12.3 Governance deadlock.** A Board unable to fill a vacancy or pass a budget for
60 days triggers automatic mediation; if unresolved 30 more days, an interim
independent Manager is appointed by the mediator solely to break ties for up to two
quarters. `[⚖ enforceability of mediator-appointed manager; alternative: AAA-appointed.]`

**12.4 Valuation disputes.** Baseball (final-offer) arbitration: each side submits one
number with a supporting appraisal; the arbitrator must pick one.

**12.5 Member-level fundamental deadlock.** If a Supermajority matter fails twice in
90 days **and** the Board certifies paralysis, any Members holding ≥ 25% may initiate
a **modified Texas shoot-out**: sealed-bid per-Unit offers, highest sealed bid wins
the right to buy the initiating/opposing bloc at that price, with a financing window
of 90 days; if the winner fails to close, the runner-up may. `[⚖ shotgun clauses
disadvantage illiquid parties; counsel to add a fairness backstop (e.g., appraisal
floor) or replace with court-free dissolution auction.]`
