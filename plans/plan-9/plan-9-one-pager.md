# plan9 — One-Pager: Governance & Equity for a Five-Founder Massachusetts LLC

**The problem in one line.** Five founders who started as equal partners have diverged — some inactive, some employees out-contributing them, a Yale ML professor informally promised "a decent share" — and nothing was ever written down, while ~15 people worked unpaid for a year.

## What the research found

1. **Quick equal splits are the documented failure mode.** 73% of founding teams split within the first month; quick-handshake equal splitters raise outside financing less often and at lower valuations (~$450K NPV at stake) — [Hellmann & Wasserman 2017, *Mgmt Science*](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mnsc.2016.2474); direction replicated in 24,194 German startups ([Mueller & Hennicke 2024](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0263237322001785)). Lab experiments show *why*: equal splits attract and enable free-riders, and **delaying the split until contribution is observable causally improves outcomes** ([Kagan, Leider & Lovejoy 2020, *Mgmt Science*](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mnsc.2019.3439)).
2. **Perceived justice — not equality — protects teams** ([Breugst et al. 2015](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0883902614000676)); free-riding disappears when individual output is identifiable ([Karau & Williams 1993 meta-analysis](http://www.psych.purdue.edu/~willia55/392F-'06/KarauWilliamsMetaAnalysisJPSP.pdf)); pay differentials are safe **only when explained by legible market inputs** ([Shaw 2014](https://www.annualreviews.org/content/journals/10.1146/annurev-orgpsych-031413-091253)) — the key to the professor's premium.
3. **Founder involvement is valuable; founder control monopoly is what costs money.** Each founder-control lever correlates with 17–22% lower valuations ([Wasserman 2017](https://sms.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/smj.2478)); co-equal co-CEOs underperform moderate hierarchy ([Krause et al. 2015](https://sms.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/smj.2325)); VCs rate team the #1 factor ([Gompers et al. 2020](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w22587/w22587.pdf)).
4. **Two legal landmines.** (a) A year of unpaid employees = accruing **mandatory-treble-damage** MA Wage Act claims with **personal officer liability**, non-waivable and not curable with equity ([Reuter v. Methuen, 2022](https://law.justia.com/cases/massachusetts/supreme-court/2022/sjc-13121.html)). (b) Massachusetts applies close-corporation "utmost good faith" fiduciary duties to LLC members ([Allison v. Eriksson, 2018](https://caselaw.findlaw.com/court/spr-jud-crt-mas-suf/1896955.html)) — any reallocation squeezing the inactive founders must be procedurally airtight.

## The design (full detail in `plan-9-design.md`; draft terms in `plan-9-operating-agreement.md`)

- **A Contribution Ledger, identical for founders and employees:** monthly Contribution Points = verified hours × published Boston-market rate for the role actually performed × **2** (non-cash), cash × **4** — the Slicing Pie engine ([slicingpie.com](https://slicingpie.com/the-magic-of-mutipliers/)), bounded by what practitioners fault it for lacking: evidence-linked logging (every hour maps to a commit/doc/meeting), 60 h/wk caps, a verification-only elected committee, and a **hard freeze**.
- **The unpaid year is settled once, through the same formula**, under tiered evidence rules (documented 100% / peer-corroborated 85% / self-report 50%), granted now while FMV is low, with 83(b)s.
- **Founder reserve (fixed requirement): 5% each, truly earned** — 16 quarterly tranches over 4 years, 1-year cliff, mechanical ≥20 h/wk activity test; missed tranches cancel **into everyone's pool**. Inactive founders keep exactly their verified past contribution, forfeit the reserve, and fade from voting power automatically — no confrontation required, no confiscation attempted.
- **The professor:** 1.0% fixed advisory grant (top of the industry-standard [FAST grid](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/joelmoxley/founder-playbook/master/IV.%20Team%20%2B%20Execution/03%20-%20Team%20Building/05%20-%20Content%20-%20Early%20Stage%20Advisors/md/01%20-%20Founder%20Institute%20-%20Advisor%20Agreements%20.webloc.md)), 24-month vesting, conditioned on **Yale COI clearance and IP licensing** (Yale, not he, may own contributed IP) — plus open Ledger enrollment at his *verifiable* market advisory rate, so his "higher rate" is honored as explained, not unexplained, dispersion.
- **Governance:** one accountable CEO (appointed and removable by the board — no entrenchment), a 3-seat elected Board of Managers (one seat reserved for a non-founder), CP-weighted member voting, a three-tier decision matrix, and supermajority + non-founder-majority protection on the Ledger formula itself. Shared leadership lives below the CEO (DRIs, decision memos) where the meta-analytic evidence actually supports it.
- **Crystallization:** at the earlier of a ≥$1M priced round, sustained breakeven, or 30 months, percentages **freeze**, the company **converts to a Delaware C-corp** (QSBS clock, SAFE/VC compatibility), a 12–15% option pool and standard 4/1 vesting take over — investors meet a boring, clean, NVCA-standard cap table with no dead equity.
- **Disputes:** negotiation → mediation → AAA arbitration (Boston); deadlock resolved by appraised fair-value buyout — **no shotgun clause** (it auctions the company to the wealthier party and is fiduciary-exposed in MA).
- **Urgent, before all of this:** counsel-led wage remediation — start lawful payroll or pause non-owner work now; the equity system supplements lawful wages, never replaces them.

## Honest caveats

No peer-reviewed study validates dynamic equity end-to-end; the case is a lattice of causal lab evidence, correlational field data, and practitioner convention — parameters like 2×/4× are convention, not science, and are labeled as such. Some sources could only be verified via live search-index confirmation (sandbox egress blocked full-text fetches); every source's verification level and evidence strength is rated in `plan-9-references.md`. We saw no other team's work at any point.
