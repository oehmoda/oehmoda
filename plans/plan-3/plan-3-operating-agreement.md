## Article V — Founder Reserved Pool (the 5% Set-Asides)

5.1 **Grant.** Each Founder is allocated a personal set-aside of **5.0%** (500,000 Units), divided into **16 equal quarterly tranches of 0.3125%** (31,250 Units), measured from the Company's founding date of [date].

5.2 **Earning conditions.** A tranche for quarter *q* is earned only if **both**: (a) *q* was an Active Service Quarter for that Founder; and (b) the Board (Founder recused) determines the Founder achieved at least "meets expectations" on a majority of that Founder's pre-set quarterly objectives; if no objectives were timely set, (b) is deemed satisfied. Earned tranches are issued as Profits Interest Units within 30 days of quarter-end certification.

5.3 **Forfeiture.** An unearned tranche is permanently forfeited and its Units transfer automatically into the Contribution Pool. No make-up, carry-forward, or Board waiver. [Rationale: anti-dead-equity by formula [C10][C13].]

5.4 **Retroactive quarters.** The four quarters preceding Restatement are tested under § 5.2(a) using the Exhibit A Retroactive Statements; § 5.2(b) is deemed satisfied for those quarters.

5.5 **Financing.** Unearned tranches continue on schedule through any financing. The Board is authorized to agree to investor-standard re-vesting or double-trigger acceleration at a Qualified Financing without further Member consent [F11]. No single-trigger acceleration.

## Article VI — Contribution Pool; Leaver Provisions

6.1 **The Contribution Plan (Exhibit A) is part of this Agreement.** Summary of its non-amendable core (amendable only as a Reserved Matter): Points = Hours × Rate(category) × 2 + Cash × 4 + Unreimbursed Expenses × 4 + approved non-cash property × 2 [C1][C8]; identical application to every Person — Founder, employee-Member, or advisor; a Person's share of the Contribution Pool = cumulative Points ÷ all cumulative Points × 62%, floating until the Freeze Event.

6.2 **Quarterly issuance.** Finalized Points convert to Profits Interest Units each quarter (Threshold = then-current FMV) so the Unit ledger tracks the current allocation; maintained in [cap-table software].

6.3 **Freeze.** At the Freeze Event, percentages fix at cumulative-Point shares; unused pool Units roll to the Future Pool; subsequent grants come only from the Future Pool on fixed schedules (default 4-year vesting, 1-year cliff, 1/48 monthly) [F10][F11].

6.4 **Guardrails** (Exhibit A, Part 3): 15% strategy-hours cap absent Board-approved deliverable; evidence-backed logging; 60-day late-log forfeit; cash cap; annual prospective-only re-benchmark of Exhibit B from [Carta compensation benchmarks; Pave Market Data] [D6][D7].

6.5 **Leaver terms (uniform for all Members)** [C2][C14]:
- **Good Leaver** (death; Disability; termination of service without Cause; resignation for Good Reason): retains all issued Units; future set-aside tranches lapse; no repurchase.
- **Bad Leaver** (termination for Cause; resignation without Good Reason before [Freeze Event + 12 months]): (i) unearned tranches lapse; (ii) Company option, exercisable 180 days, to repurchase (A) earned set-aside Units at the lower of FMV and $[nominal]; (B) Units derived from non-cash Points at $[nominal]; (C) Units derived from cash Points at 1× the cash contributed.
- Repurchase terms and exercise must be uniform across Members [Donahue equal-opportunity [E4]]. [Counsel flags: nominal-price repurchase vs. utmost-good-faith duties [E4][E6]; coordination with the non-waivable § 36 resignation right — this Article regulates economics of exit, not the right to resign [E2].]

6.6 **Dispute of any Point, rate, level, or leaver determination** exclusively via Article XII (Committee → final-offer arbitration).

## Exhibit A — Contribution Plan (operative summary)

**Part 1 — Formula.** Points(p,q) = Σc [Hours(p,c,q) × Rate(c) × 2] + Cash×4 + UnreimbursedExpenses×4 + ApprovedProperty×2. At-risk only: any compensated fraction of an hour earns no Points (at-risk fraction = max(0, 1 − pay ÷ Rate(c))) [C1][C8]. Pool share = cumulative Points ÷ total Points × 62%, floating until Freeze.

**Part 2 — Rate Schedule** = Exhibit B; the rate attaches to the *work performed*, not the person; per-person category levels set annually (CEO proposal → Committee review → Board).

**Part 3 — Guardrails.** Strategy/vision ≤15% of a person's quarterly hours absent Board-approved deliverable; management hours require named reports/deliverables; evidence links expected (repos, calendars, artifacts); logs due weekly, certified monthly (CEO countersign; Independent Manager countersigns the CEO's), forfeited if >60 days late; cash >$[25,000]/quarter needs Board pre-approval for the 4× multiplier.

**Part 4 — Cycle.** Quarterly Committee statement to all Members; 30-day objection window; unobjected = final; objections → Art. XII(c). Quarterly Unit issuance with fresh Threshold + protective 83(b) [A9].

**Part 5 — Retroactive true-up (one time).** Retroactive Contribution Statements due 45 days post-Restatement covering the pre-Restatement period, quarter by quarter, same formula and multipliers, evidence-backed (git history, PR reviews, releases, calendars, documents, bank records), sworn; two-Member counter-certification; publication + 30-day objections; Committee resolution; unresolved → final-offer arbitration [G16].
